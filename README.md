## 🔭 What I am building right now

Full stack engineer at **BioBrain Insights (Tooliqa Innovations)**, working where backend engineering meets applied AI:

- **InstaQual** — Node.js pipelines that take raw qualitative research interviews through speech-to-text and LLMs, and come out the other side as structured themes, sentiments and pull-quotes for client-ready reports.
- **Market data at volume** — real-time and historical pipelines for NASDAQ and NYSE feeds, normalized and analyzed on Docker Swarm.
- **Making LLMs behave** — iterative prompt engineering, structured output workflows, RAG and MCP, with accuracy measured rather than assumed.

Before this: Project Lead on the B2B Marketplace at **Austere Systems**, and frontend engineering at **Fraktal IT Innovation**.
